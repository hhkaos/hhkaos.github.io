Geospatial projects ideas

Excuses to learn geospatial This is just a compilation of ideas that would provide an excuse to learn and practices ... where I ideas or projects on how geospatial knowledge can be used for fun or meaningful things:

Personal use or share with friends​

📍 Geotagged memories & travel mapping​

  • Map your geolocated (geotagged) holiday photos.
  • Visualize locations where you have been (e.g. where I have been).
  • Create visual stories using animated videos from tracking outdoor activities (like relive.cc).
  • Build a 360º video tour of places you like or you’ve visited.
  • Map your "wish list" of dream travel destinations.

🤔 Decision-making & analysis​

  • Find the best location to buy/rent a house or stay in a hotel combining geospatial data.
  • Analyze your Google Maps location history to understand movement patterns (or know how many times you have been in X) example.
  • Map to help choose the best school zones for your kids.
  • Create a school guide with important details on schools nearby.

🐶 Pet projects & experimental apps​

Event & personal mapping projects​

  • Create a custom wedding map to help guests find venues, accommodations, and activities (e.g. my own).
  • Make a personal audio guide using AI and Wikipedia API to narrate geolocated stories.
  • Help conference attendees to find other attendees to do ride-sharing
  • Build a Geoguess/Geovote application (e.g., a "Geoporra" game).
  • Create a fun Santa Claus tracker for the holidays.
  • Create a maps of tech communities, conferences, and meetups around the world (example)

🗺 Exploration, Fun & Curiosity​

  • Explore historical maps and city transformations with old satellite imagery (example.

  • Discover radio stations by location (e.g., Radio garden).

  • Play Geogames to learn about world geography interactively.

  • Use educational digital atlas to help your kids

  • TV & movie location mapping (e.g., Game of Thrones, La casa de papel filming spots).

  • Fantasy map generator - – create maps for fictional worlds

  • Explore +20K satellites in orbit using interactive maps.

  • Monitor Earth Observation Data: wildfires, air quality, climate change.

  • Create animated gifs with wayback imagery

Sharing Knowledge & Community Impact​

Share knowledge​

  • Share recomendations (e.g Victoriano)
  • Share favourite hiking routes

Help your community​

  • Map to help you choose school zones
  • Create a turistic guide of your city (Nerja interactive guide)
  • Create open data of your neighbourhood from imagery with Mapillary

Humanitarian / People care​

  • Participate in Mapathons
  • InfoRiesgo (idea)
  • Social good hackathons / MLH
