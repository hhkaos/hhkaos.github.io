# Geospatial projects ideas

Excuses to learn geospatial
This is just a compilation of ideas that would provide an excuse to learn and practices ... where I
ideas or projects on how geospatial knowledge can be used for fun or meaningful things:

## Personal use or share with friends

### 📍 Geotagged memories & travel mapping

* Map your geolocated (geotagged) holiday photos.
* Visualize locations where you have been (e.g. [where I have been](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/1/edit?mid=1Vaeh6GY9VzbGgMJI6kXZ79PlgUg&usp=sharing)).
* Create visual stories using animated videos from tracking outdoor activities (like relive.cc).
* [Build a **360º video tour** of places you like or you’ve visited](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HpyGWGeZ6bI&ab_channel=GeoDevelopers).
* Map your "**wish list**" of dream travel destinations.

### 🤔 Decision-making & analysis

* Find the best location to buy/rent a house or stay in a hotel combining geospatial data.
* **Analyze your Google Maps location history** to understand movement patterns (or know how many times you have been in X) [example](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paullgibbs_30daymapchallenge-webdev-gis-activity-7259459809349939202-Tk3y).
* Map to help **choose the best school zones** for your kids.
* Create a [school guide](https://devsummit-demo.maps.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=b9cc37821a8340028f57fbb5c1ba1622) with important details on schools nearby.

## 🐶 Pet projects & experimental apps

### Event & personal mapping projects 

* Create a custom **wedding map** to help guests find venues, accommodations, and activities (e.g. [my own](https://web.archive.org/web/20181202055117/http://www.bodorrioenvera.es/#lugares-de-interes)).
* Make a personal audio guide using AI and Wikipedia API to narrate geolocated stories.
* Help conference attendees to find other attendees to do ride-sharing
* Build a Geoguess/Geovote application (e.g., a "[Geoporra](https://youtu.be/U039pAvPHoc?si=swyYZ9XwdzSFeWsE)" game).
* Create a fun Santa Claus tracker for the holidays.
* Create a maps of tech communities, conferences, and meetups around the world ([example](https://groups.google.com/g/community-builders-es/c/agm4LEFrZco/m/KtPN4peRAgAJ))


### 🗺 Exploration, Fun & Curiosity

* Explore historical maps and city transformations with [old satellite imagery](https://pnoa.ign.es/pnoa-imagen/vuelos-y-ortofotos-historicas) ([example](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1zYoz2VTfWmHGQA_Fnp1SgxnzgJyVqOaMBORfUcDt0pA/edit?usp=sharing).
* Discover radio stations by location (e.g., [Radio garden](https://radio.garden/visit/malaga/xdFoGP7o)).
* Play [Geogames](https://geovoluntarios.github.io/geonight/index.html?lng=en-US) to learn about world geography interactively.
* Use [educational digital atlas](https://atlas-escolar.maps.arcgis.com/home/index.html) to help your kids
* TV & movie location mapping (e.g., [Game of Thrones](https://cbcni.maps.arcgis.com/apps/MapJournal/index.html?appid=59221d543bbe4b119ccdee8dc4a7363d), [La casa de papel](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/3db23b48d9ec44c590929b2e5e9ac067) filming spots).
* [Fantasy map generator](https://azgaar.github.io/Fantasy-Map-Generator/) -  – create maps for fictional worlds
* [Explore +20K satellites in orbit](https://geoxc-apps.bd.esri.com/space/satellite-explorer/) using interactive maps.
* Monitor [Earth Observation Data](https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/en/apps/): wildfires, air quality, climate change.
* Create animated gifs with [wayback imagery](https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/wayback/#active=34007&mapCenter=-3.70931%2C40.43061%2C12)
