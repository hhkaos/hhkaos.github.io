## Currently

At work, I am trying to help my colleagues share more knowledge and code in the open, participate in technical events, appear on podcasts, and create materials that help and inspire developers working with our technologies.

I am learning about the [IndieWeb](https://indieweb.org/) and tending to this website as a more independent, durable place for my writing, notes, identity, and experiments on the open web.

In communities, I am writing a book about the history of technical communities in Spain and trying to help move OpenTechSpec forward from [Community Builders](https://communitybuilders.dev/).

Personally, I am researching my family history and building a family tree.

## Open Threads

- Exploring how AI can help turn ideas into useful artifacts without losing personal judgment

## Less Priority Now

I have stepped away from the school parents' association and from my collaboration on an AI and education podcast so I can spend more time exercising and getting more involved in local communities in Almeria.

That does not mean I have disconnected from the school: I will keep collaborating whenever I can contribute something useful.

I have also left 3D printing a bit unattended lately. I am still interested in it, but it is not at the center right now.
