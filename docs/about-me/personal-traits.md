# Personal traits

I don't think of these as strengths or weaknesses — any trait can play for or against you depending on the context.

After a lot of reflection (and contrasting with the feedback I've received over the years), these are the traits that feel most defining to me:

- **Driven**: high capacity for work — when something matters to me, I keep pushing until it exists.
- **Polyvalent**: I've operated across very different areas (development, product, community, communication, business, strategy...), which lets me adapt fast and fill in when responsibilities aren't clearly assigned.
- **Vision**: I tend to spot gaps and problems before they're formally on the radar.
- **Bias to materialization**: I turn strategic ideas into tangible artifacts (MVPs, frameworks, initiatives).
- **Ownership**: I take responsibility for initiatives and can generate traction without depending on formal authority.
- **Systemic thinking**: I see how engineering, product, community, and business connect, and I work to reduce friction between disciplines.
- **Unblocking**: turning ambiguous or abstract situations into operational clarity through execution comes naturally to me.
- **Patient persistence**: when there's resistance, I know how to wait, iterate, and pick my battles without abandoning the direction altogether.
- **Designing for scale**: I try to create initiatives that others can adopt, expand, and make their own.
- **Communicator**: I can produce content in almost any format — articles, talks, videos, podcasts.
- **Communities**: years of experience building, leading, and participating in them (see [open culture](open-culture.md)).
- **Compounding capital**: I keep, organize, and reuse ideas, materials, and frameworks so that work doesn't get lost and can evolve over time.
- **Open to feedback**: I generally welcome criticism of my work and use it to adjust course.
- **Idea-flow / scattered**: I'm creative and ideas flow easily — sometimes too easily. I might start the day asking ChatGPT about mint and end up rewriting my professional profile five hours later.

> **Note**: this is a self-perception. If you know me and feel something is missing or off, I'd genuinely like to hear it.
